Same prompt + relaxed watchdog env vars → full correct delivery in 178s. Reported upstream: [hermes-agent#112909](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/issues/112909).
3. **Correction policy applied.** The W37 ui-build cell is restated as ✓ 12/12 (infra kill, not a model verdict); the makeup is labeled at the cell, not in a footnote. **luna's W37 ui-build zero-delivery shows the same watchdog signature — suspected wrongful, NOT re-measured, NOT corrected.** A luna re-test under relaxed watchdogs is owed; until then its 15/23 stands as published.
4. **Two W37 harness walls now have real scores.** Both verify cases that ∅'d on the 1800s cap delivered today under the per-case 3600s cap (7/15 and 3/12) — both still fail, so the case-level tally is untouched, but the cells are real deliveries now, not infrastructure blanks.
5. **Fourth perfect score on the ui-build case.** A-d9b79b46's published perfect runs: glm-5.3-flash (Ollama), astra (W36, three bands), hy4-preview-f (WorkBuddy, W37 addendum), and now sol — the first for this lane.
